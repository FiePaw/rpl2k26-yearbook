FFmpeg Encoding Parameters

Video codec: H.264 (libx264)

Audio codec: AAC @ 128kbps

Encoding Preset: Medium (balance antara speed dan quality)

Quality Level (CRF): 23 (default good balance)

Contoh Command:

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 \
  -c:v libx264 \
  -preset medium \
  -crf 23 \
  -scale=1280:720 \
  -movflags +faststart \
  -c:a aac -b:a 128k \
  output_720p.mp4
